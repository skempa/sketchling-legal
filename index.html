Sketchling privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Sketchling turns a drawing into a creature with a name, a dex entry and a 3D body you can stand in your room. This page says what information the app handles, where it goes and how long it is kept. It is written to be read.

No account

Sketchling has no sign-up. When the app is first opened it creates a random identifier for that install. The identifier is not your name, email, phone number or Apple ID, and it cannot be used to identify you outside the app. It exists so the service can count how many creatures an install has made.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • Your drawing (or a photo you choose), the sentence describing it and the install identifier are sent to Sketchling's server when you ask for a creature's name and dex entry, and when you ask for its 3D body.
  • The server passes the drawing and description to two providers that do the generation: Anthropic (the creature's name, types and dex text) and Tripo3D (concept art and the 3D model). They receive the image and text needed to do that work and nothing else about you.
  • Generated concept art and 3D models are stored on the server for up to 7 days so your phone can download them, then deleted. Your drawing is deleted from the server as soon as the 3D body is finished.

What stays on your phone

Your creatures — drawings, names, stats, concept art and 3D models — live in the app on your device. They are not uploaded for safekeeping or syncing, and deleting a creature deletes its files. Photos you take in the room view are saved only where you choose to share or save them.

The camera

The camera is used to place your creature in your room and, if you choose, to photograph a drawing on paper. Camera images are processed on the device and are not sent anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. Sketchling's server keeps a record of whether an install has an active subscription and how many creatures it has made this period, in order to provide what you paid for. Sketchling never sees your payment details.

What Sketchling does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of your data for anyone else's purposes.

Content and safety

Drawings and descriptions are checked for suitability for all ages before a creature is generated. Requests that fail that check are declined and are not used for anything else.

Children

Sketchling collects no personal information and has no accounts or messaging, so it can be used by anyone. If you are a parent with a question about your child's use of the app, write to the address below.

Deleting everything

Deleting the app deletes your creatures from your device. Anything on the server tied to your install (generated files awaiting download, and the usage count) expires within 7 days of your last activity or can be removed on request.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes and the app's Settings screen links to the new version.
